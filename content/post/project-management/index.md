## Structural Mechanics, Vibration & Damage Detection

Structures in engineering — from bridges and buildings to aircraft and wind turbines — 
are constantly subjected to dynamic loads, vibrations, and environmental degradation. 
Understanding how structures **respond, deform, and fail** is fundamental to safe and 
efficient design.

### Structural Mechanics
Structural mechanics forms the foundation of engineering analysis, dealing with how 
structures carry and transfer loads. Key topics include:

- **Stress and strain analysis** — understanding deformation under static and dynamic loads
- **Finite Element Analysis (FEA)** — numerical simulation of complex structural behavior
- **Composite and advanced materials** — lightweight structures for aerospace and civil applications

### Structural Dynamics & Vibration
All real structures vibrate. Managing and understanding vibration is critical for:

- **Modal analysis** — identifying natural frequencies, mode shapes, and damping ratios
- **System identification** — extracting dynamic models from experimental data (ARX, DMD, Koopman)
- **Vibration control** — active and passive strategies to suppress unwanted oscillations
- **Seismic and wind response** — protecting structures against environmental dynamic loads

### Structural Damage Detection
Early detection of damage prevents catastrophic failure. Our approach uses:

- **Ultrasonic guided waves (Lamb waves)** — detecting cracks, delaminations, and corrosion
- **Vibration-based methods** — changes in natural frequencies and mode shapes as damage indicators
- **Data-driven diagnostics** — machine learning for automated damage classification
- **Sparse sensing** — optimal sensor networks for maximum structural coverage

Together, these three areas form a comprehensive framework for **structural health monitoring (SHM)** — 
enabling real-time, autonomous assessment of critical infrastructure.
