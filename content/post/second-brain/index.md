## Inverted Pendulum Control

The inverted pendulum is a classic benchmark problem in control systems engineering. 
It consists of a pendulum attached to a cart, where the goal is to keep the pendulum 
balanced in the upright (unstable) equilibrium position by applying a horizontal force to the cart.
