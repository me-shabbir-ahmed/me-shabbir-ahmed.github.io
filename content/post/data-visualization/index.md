## Lithium-Ion Battery Aging

Lithium-ion batteries are the dominant energy storage technology in electric vehicles, 
portable electronics, and grid-scale energy systems. As batteries age, their capacity 
and power capability degrade — making accurate **State of Health (SoH)** and 
**State of Charge (SoC)** estimation difficult.
