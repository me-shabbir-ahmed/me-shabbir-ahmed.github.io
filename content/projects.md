        Our research spans structural health monitoring and structural dynamics, developing algorithms for battery management systems, and understanding control systems in general.

        ---

        <h3 style="color: #2563eb;"> Damage Detection in Structures</h3>

        We develop **data-driven algorithms** for detecting damage within a structure. We have utilized **ultrasonic guided waves (Lamb waves)** and **random vibrations** for this particular task. By combining time series analysis techniques (ARX/VARX type models) with physics-based wave propagation principles, our methods enable robust detection of cracks and damage with proper uncertainty bounds.

        ---

        <h3 style="color: #16a34a;"> Lithium-Ion Battery Degradation Monitoring and State Estimation</h3>

        We model the dynamics of lithium-ion battery and develop efficient algorithms for estimating **State of Charge (SoC)** and **State of Health (SoH)** of lithium-ion batteries under aging effects.

        ---

        <h3 style="color: #dc2626;"> Robotics and Controls</h3>

        We explore different control strategies for dynamical systems, develop novel methods for system identification and perform long-horizon prediction of robotic systems.
